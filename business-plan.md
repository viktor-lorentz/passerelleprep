# Bilingual Elite Prep + Admissions — Business Plan (v2)

Partners: Viktor (USC ECE; dev/ops; rocketry+underwater-robotics; published Android app; restaurant ops;
camp counselor; fluent French) + Andrew (top SAT/PSAT/ACT; prior tutoring; fluent Chinese).

## Honest frame
1-on-1 tutoring = good income ($2-5k/mo each), NOT wealth. Wealth = leverage: other people's hours
(agency) + software (scale) + high-ticket admissions consulting. This plan starts as tutoring for cash
and is built to become those three things.

## Core moat: two language communities, one per founder
- Andrew -> Mandarin / San Gabriel Valley Chinese families. Big, dense, high-WTP, free reach (WeChat + Xiaohongshu).
- Viktor -> French / Francophone LA (Lycee International de LA, French expat families) + USC English-speaking families.
Uncopyable: requires both founders' specific languages.

## Three stacked revenue layers
Layer 1 - TEST PREP (front door / cash now): digital SAT/ACT 1-on-1 + small group. Both recently aced
  the current adaptive format. $70/hr intro -> $100-120 with results. "$999 10-session Digital SAT Program."
Layer 2 - STEM-PORTFOLIO + ADMISSIONS CONSULTING (the money / high ticket): admissions packages run
  thousands+. Edge = Viktor's rocketry/robotics/USC-eng/published-app resume -> sell standout STEM
  portfolios; camp-counselor background -> run premium kids' build workshops. Test-prep families convert up.
Layer 3 - SOFTWARE + AGENCY (scale beyond our hours / the rich part): Viktor builds auto-diagnostic +
  adaptive practice + bilingual (EN/中文/FR) parent dashboard + score reports. Then (a) recruit other USC
  top-scorers as 1099 tutors under the brand (margin on their hours), (b) license/spin out the platform.

## Roles (full stacks mapped)
- Andrew: lead verbal tutor; Chinese parent sales/closing; SGV WeChat/RED engine.
- Viktor French: Francophone-family channel + French admissions comms.
- Viktor dev: platform, score reports, booking, scale layer.
- Viktor rocketry/robotics/USC: STEM-portfolio consulting + premium build workshops (high ticket).
- Viktor camp counselor: kids' group programs + parent trust.
- Viktor restaurant ops: business ops, scheduling.
- Both USC students: credibility, campus networks, tutor-recruiting pool.

## Timing (Oct 2026) - launching now is prime, not late
- SAT: Dec 5 2026 live; then Mar 6 / May 1 / Jun 5 2027. ACT: Dec 12 2026; then Feb 27 / Apr 10 / Jun 12 2027.
- Junior cohort preps now-Feb for spring tests ("start by Jan of junior year").

## Pricing
- Intro $70/hr -> $100-120 with results. Packages preferred ($999 / 10 sessions). Small-group = 3-5x hourly.
- $500/mo = 1-2 students (floor). Cap 6-8 students solo = $2.5-4.5k/mo split. Real money is Layers 2-3.

## Go-to-market (free)
WeChat SGV/USC Chinese-parent groups; Xiaohongshu; Francophone LA (LILA, expat groups); Nextdoor; USC networks;
referrals (reward 1 free session/referral). Hook = free 30-min digital diagnostic + score report (Viktor builds).
Payment: WeChat Pay / Zelle / Venmo. Wyzant = supplemental leads only (keeps 75% minus 9% fee).

## Phased roadmap
1. Weeks 1-4 (cash): Layer 1 dual-channel. Target 6-8 students, ~$2.5-4k/mo.
2. Months 2-4 (margin): small-group cohorts + STEM-portfolio/admissions upsell to existing families.
3. Months 4-9 (scale): ship platform; recruit 2-3 USC top-scorers as brand tutors.
4. Months 9+ (wealth): license/productize software; expand admissions arm (highest margin).

## Validation bar (2 weeks) - so this isn't a time sink
Book 5 free diagnostics + convert 2 to paid packages. If <5 diagnostics from free channels, fix positioning/channel.

## This-week checklist
1. Lock name + positioning + package pricing.
2. Bilingual (EN/中文/FR) one-page site + Calendly (Viktor).
3. Free diagnostic + score-report offer.
4. Andrew posts 3-5 WeChat groups + 1 Xiaohongshu; Viktor posts Francophone groups + Nextdoor + USC.
5. Referral reward live.
6. Accept WeChat Pay/Zelle/Venmo.

## Risks
- Layer 1 alone = income, not wealth; the leverage layers (2-3) are the point.
- French channel = differentiator, not main engine; validate AFTER Chinese proves out.
- Both on USC eng teams -> capacity risk; plan only works if Viktor builds Layer 3 + they recruit tutors,
  else it's just two jobs. Cap enrollment early to protect results/referrals.
- SGV saturated with prep centers -> win on recency + 1-on-1 + price + bilingual parent trust, not brand.
- Parents doubt freshman tutors -> score proof, diagnostic report, rapport.
- Minors/legal -> sessions online/recorded; written partner split now; LLC later.
